### **Section 1: Password and Credential Security**

1.1 Password Complexity Requirements:  
To protect company and client data, all employee passwords for accessing company systems (email, VPN, internal applications) must meet the following criteria:

* **Minimum Length:** 12 characters.  
* **Character Composition:** Must include at least one of each of the following four character types:  
  * Uppercase letters (A-Z)  
  * Lowercase letters (a-z)  
  * Numbers (0-9)  
  * Special characters (e.g., \! @ \# $ % ^ & \*)

**1.2 Password Expiration and History:**

* Passwords **must be changed every 90 days**. Automated system notifications will be sent to employees 14 days and 3 days prior to the expiration date.  
* The system will enforce a password history policy that prevents the reuse of the last **five (5)** passwords.

**1.3 Account Lockout Policy:**

* User accounts will be automatically locked for a period of **30 minutes** after **five (5) consecutive unsuccessful login attempts**.  
* After the lockout period, the user may attempt to log in again. If a user is locked out and requires immediate access, they must contact the IT Help Desk for verification and an account reset.

### **Section 2: Data Handling and Acceptable Use**

2.1 Confidential Information:  
Employees are prohibited from sharing confidential, proprietary, or client-sensitive information with unauthorized individuals or third parties. All sensitive data must be stored on company-managed network drives or approved cloud services. Transferring sensitive data via personal email or unencrypted personal devices is strictly forbidden.  
2.2 Use of Company Equipment:  
All company-provided equipment, including laptops, mobile phones, and software, is intended for business use. Incidental personal use is permitted but should not interfere with job responsibilities or violate any company policies. Employees should have no expectation of privacy when using company equipment.
