### **Section 1: Vacation Policy (Paid Time Off \- PTO)**

1.1 Eligibility:  
All full-time, salaried employees are eligible for paid vacation leave. PTO is available for use after the successful completion of a 90-day introductory period.  
1.2 Accrual and Allowance:  
Eligible full-time employees are granted 20 days of paid vacation per calendar year. This allowance is provided in a lump sum on January 1st of each year. For employees hired mid-year, the allowance will be prorated based on their start date.  
1.3 Requesting Vacation:  
All vacation requests must be submitted through the official HR portal at least two (2) weeks prior to the requested time off. For requests of five (5) or more consecutive days, employees should provide at least thirty (30) days' notice. All requests are subject to approval by the employee's direct manager and will be evaluated based on business needs and team coverage.  
1.4 Carryover Policy:  
Employees are encouraged to use their full vacation allowance each year. A maximum of five (5) unused vacation days may be carried over into the first quarter (January 1 \- March 31\) of the following year. Any carried-over days not used by March 31st will be forfeited.

### **Section 2: Sick Leave Policy**

2.1 Accrual:  
Sick leave is a separate benefit from paid vacation. Full-time employees accrue sick leave at a rate of one (1) day per month, totaling twelve (12) days per year. Unused sick leave may be carried over, up to a maximum balance of 30 days.  
2.2 Permitted Usage:  
Sick leave can be used for an employee's own illness, injury, or medical/dental appointments. It may also be used to care for an immediate family member (spouse, child, or parent) who is ill.  
2.3 Notification Procedures:  
Employees must notify their direct manager via email or phone as soon as possible, but no later than the start of their scheduled workday, if they are unable to work due to illness. For absences lasting three (3) or more consecutive business days, the company reserves the right to require a doctor's note upon the employee's return to work.
